# Working paper

`main.tex` is the English working paper on the stage F experiment (contract Section 20), its exploratory supplements, the targeted-audit experiment (contract Section 21), the assurance experiment (contract Section 22), the learning-firm redo (contract Section 23) and the joint robustness test (contract Section 24). The version of 20 October 2026 is the one first posted on SSRN; the version of 23 October 2026 adds the assurance experiment, the learning-firm redo and the joint robustness test, and describes the mean-reversion rates as data-motivated sensitivity values rather than upper bounds. Red bracketed text in the PDF marks placeholders; none remain.

Build from this directory:

```sh
python make_figures.py
latexmk -pdf main.tex
```

`make_figures.py` reads only committed analysis files under `reports/`. `composition.py` derives the composition shares quoted in the introduction and in Sections 6.3, 6.5, 8 and 9 (shares of all inflating decisions by firm size, and the share made by firms that already carry undetected inflation) from the `describe` blocks of `audits_v1_analysis.json` and `assurance_v1_analysis.json`; they are descriptive, computed on 25 October 2026 after the experiments. Its last number, the share of assured firms' inflating decisions at the year's lowest-quality provider without natural decay (97.4%, 12,262 of 12,594), needs the assurance experiment's local archive and uses the code of prediction T5, which on ALL_F16 reproduces 46,489 of 47,953. The committed figures were drawn with matplotlib 3.11.1, the version in `requirements-lock.txt`. The two tables of the targeted-audit section are typed from `reports/disclosure_targeted_audits_20261020/audits_v1_tables.md` and `audits_v1_analysis.json`; the intervals for V_hidden − B and J_hidden − B come from the run's `summary.json`, which stays local, and match the report's README. The two tables of the assurance section and its numbers are typed from `reports/disclosure_assurance_20261021/README.md` and `assurance_v1_analysis.json`; the numbers of the learning-firm redo come from `reports/disclosure_learning_redo_20261021/README.md` and `redo_v1_analysis.json`; the joint-test rows of the robustness table and the numbers of the paragraph "Both assumptions together" come from `reports/disclosure_joint_robustness_20261023/README.md` and `joint_v1_analysis.json`.
