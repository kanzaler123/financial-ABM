# Working paper

`main.tex` is the English working paper on the stage F experiment (contract Section 20), its exploratory supplements, the targeted-audit experiment (contract Section 21), the assurance experiment (contract Section 22), the learning-firm redo (contract Section 23) and the joint robustness test (contract Section 24). The version of 20 October 2026 is the one first posted on SSRN; the version of 23 October 2026 adds the assurance experiment, the learning-firm redo and the joint robustness test, and describes the mean-reversion rates as data-motivated sensitivity values rather than upper bounds. Red bracketed text in the PDF marks placeholders; none remain.

Build from this directory:

```sh
python make_figures.py
latexmk -pdf main.tex
```

`make_figures.py` reads only committed analysis files under `reports/`. The committed figures were drawn with matplotlib 3.11.1, the version in `requirements-lock.txt`. The two tables of the targeted-audit section are typed from `reports/disclosure_targeted_audits_20261020/audits_v1_tables.md` and `audits_v1_analysis.json`; the intervals for V_hidden − B and J_hidden − B come from the run's `summary.json`, which stays local, and match the report's README. The two tables of the assurance section and its numbers are typed from `reports/disclosure_assurance_20261021/README.md` and `assurance_v1_analysis.json`; the numbers of the learning-firm redo come from `reports/disclosure_learning_redo_20261021/README.md` and `redo_v1_analysis.json`; the joint-test rows of the robustness table and the numbers of the paragraph "Both assumptions together" come from `reports/disclosure_joint_robustness_20261023/README.md` and `joint_v1_analysis.json`. The subsection on the in-sample financing premium (Appendix A.5, contract Section 26) is typed from `reports/disclosure_financing_premium_20261027/README.md` and `reports/disclosure_stage_i_20261027/README.md`; the numbers of its table come from `stage_i_v2_analysis.json` in the same directory. The subsection on investor response (Appendix A.6, contract Section 27) is typed from `reports/disclosure_investor_response_20261028/README.md` and `estimates.json`; the model's counterparts come from `model_moments.json` in the same directory.
